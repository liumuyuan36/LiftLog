Authoring source and static exports
----------------------------------
human.json is the locally derived mesh, rig and normalized skin-weight data used by renderer.js.
Original data paths (all CC0 per file headers/license fields):
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/makehuman/data/rigs/default.mhskel
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/master/makehuman/data/rigs/default_weights.mhw
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/tree/master/makehuman/data/targets/macrodetails
Derivation: equal African/Caucasian adult male young targets, 0.8 universal male young maxmuscle averageweight target; body surface and helper-tights surface only. Shorts clipped to pelvis-relative y [-0.27,0.13]. Four largest skin weights per vertex normalized.
Renderer: pinned three@0.180.0 from https://www.npmjs.com/package/three
Run from project root: node artwork/server.mjs
Open http://127.0.0.1:52991/artwork/studio.html and click export. Export contains WebP images, manifest and projected hit polygons. dist/media/body3d and dist/art-assets.js are generated outputs. No original photographs or paid exercise assets are used.
The visual regions are approximate product navigation masks, not anatomical segmentation. The pose configurations are illustrative and have not been independently reviewed by a fitness professional.
